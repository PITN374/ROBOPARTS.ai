1. Purpose

Establish the evidence requirements, document-control procedures, engineering validation gates, acceptance criteria, and implementation roadmap for ROBOPARTS.ai.

The objective is to ensure that product selection, supplier qualification, technical claims, procurement decisions, and software implementation remain traceable.

E1 — Public Reference Evidence
Public product catalog.

E2 — Engineering Selection Evidence

E3 — Controlled Engineering Evidence

E4 — Sample Validation Evidence
Sample inspection record.
Measured torque and speed.

E5 — Production and Quality Evidence
Lot-specific test reports.
Outgoing quality control records.
Inspection certificates.
Manufacturing change records.
Production consistency evidence.
Shipment and traceability records.

These categories describe evidence maturity, not an automatic guarantee of authenticity or quality.

3. Evidence Availability Rules

Public selection information may be used for early comparison.

Controlled drawings and CAD should be requested after the candidate model and project scope are established.

Factory-floor photographs, teardown images, and lot-specific test reports must not be represented as actual project evidence until genuine source assets have been obtained and their provenance established.

Synthetic illustrations must be identified as illustrative.

Missing documentation must be recorded as missing rather than invented.

Define required:

Operating cycle.

Gate 02 — Mechanical Envelope

Confirm:

Flange.
Mounting interface.
Output interface.
Connector position.

Gate 03 — Electrical and Control

Confirm:

Current limits.
Driver architecture.
Encoder.
Brake.
Communication protocol.
Firmware.
Harness pinout.
Connector.

Requirement: The sample must be integrable with the intended bench-test configuration.

Gate 04 — Performance and Thermal

Thermal rise.
Duty cycle.
Cooling path.
Overload duration.
Recovery behavior.
Backlash.
Stiffness.

Requirement: Continuous operating suitability must be assessed independently of peak torque.

Gate 05 — Sample Acceptance

Define the pass/fail criteria before sample release.

Potential measurements:

Dimensional fit.
Continuous torque.
Peak torque.
Speed.
Temperature rise.
Backlash.
Noise.
Force response.
Repeatability.
Brake behavior.
Communication stability.

The actual acceptance values must be project-specific.

Gate 06 — Pilot Continuity

Confirm:

Revision control.
Inspection requirements.
Packaging.
Delivery.
Incoterm.
Production quantity.
Change notification.
Lot traceability.

A successful sample is not automatic proof of production readiness.

6. Five-Stage RFQ Decision Matrix
Stage	Buyer input	Supplier evidence	Hold condition
01 Fit	Joint, envelope, quantity	Model and drawing	Dimensions unresolved
02 Duty	Continuous/peak torque, speed, cycle	Performance and thermal records	Peak-only selection
03 Interface	Voltage, protocol, encoder, brake	Wiring and compatibility	Control stack unresolved
04 Sample	Defined acceptance test	Inspection and test evidence	No pass/fail criteria
05 Pilot	Volume, inspection, delivery	OQC and change control	No repeat-lot controls

No-go conditions must be recorded explicitly.

9. Implementation Status

Use these definitions:

PROPOSED — Identified as a future requirement.

DESIGNED — Technical design documented.

IMPLEMENTED — Source code or service exists.

TESTED — Defined tests have been executed.

INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED — Appropriate independent verification has been completed.

DEPRECATED — No longer recommended or supported.

Do not mark a capability implemented merely because its specification exists.

13. Change-Control Policy

New documentation must not silently overwrite established definitions.

Each material change should record:

Document affected.
Previous version.
New version.
Description of change.
Reason.
Source of information.
Approval status.
Dependencies.
Validation required.

Conflicting supplier or engineering information should remain traceable until resolved.

14. Final Development Principle

ROBOPARTS.ai must progress from catalog discovery to engineering qualification, sample validation, supplier qualification, and procurement with evidence appropriate to each stage.

The purpose of the system is not simply to collect product information.

It is to establish an auditable relationship between:

IDENTITY
SPECIFICATION
SOURCE EVIDENCE
ENGINEERING SUITABILITY
SUPPLIER CAPABILITY
SAMPLE PERFORMANCE
QUALITY
COMMERCIAL TERMS
PROCUREMENT DECISION
LIFECYCLE HISTORY

The same evidence-driven principle must apply throughout the PITN.ai ecosystem.

This document establishes the proposed baseline and does not represent completed implementation or verified supplier qualification.
