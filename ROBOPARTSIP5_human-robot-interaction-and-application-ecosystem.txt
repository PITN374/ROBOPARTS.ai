19. HUMAN-CENTERED VISION
The long-term opportunity is to make robots understandable not only as machines, but as complete intelligent systems.

A robot profile could eventually answer:

What is this robot?

What can it do?

How does it communicate?

How does it behave?

What does it remember?

What tasks can it perform?

What are its limitations?

What hardware does it require?

How well does it perform?

How does it adapt?

What applications is it suitable for?
This is the purpose of combining standardized robot description, intelligent-agent behavior, and physical robotics infrastructure.
