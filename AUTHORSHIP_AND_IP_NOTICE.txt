6. ROBOPARTS.ai ORIGINAL CONCEPT

RoboParts.ai is intended to extend conventional robotics component catalogs.

The conceptual foundation is that a robot is an interconnected assembly of physical components.

Those components contribute capabilities to the overall robotic system.

The platform therefore seeks to connect:

ROBOT | v CAPABILITIES | v REQUIRED HARDWARE | v ROBOPARTS.ai | v COMPONENTS | v MANUFACTURERS | v SUPPLIERS | v INTEGRATORS | v MAINTENANCE | v LIFECYCLE ACCOUNTABILITY

The longer-term vision includes digital identity and accountability for individual components assigned to individual robotic platforms.

7. DIGITAL IDENTITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY CONCEPT

An important element of the RoboParts.ai concept is the distinction between a generic commercial product and an individual physical component.

The intended framework includes:

Individual component identification.
Serialized component registration.
Robot-specific BOM relationships.
Installation records.
Component replacement history.
Manufacturer and supplier relationships.
Maintenance history.
Recall identification.
Configuration history.
Capability relationships.

The objective is to establish a structured digital record for each designated component and its relationship to the robotic system in which it is installed.

8. ROBOAGENTIC AND ROBO TRAITS INTEROPERABILITY

The broader PITN.ai ecosystem may connect hardware identity with robotic characteristics and intelligent-agent systems.

Conceptually:

PHYSICAL COMPONENTS | v ROBOPARTS.ai | v VERIFIED HARDWARE CAPABILITIES | v ROBOT PROFILE | v ROBOTRAITS | v ROBOAGENTIC.ai | v INTELLIGENT ROBOTIC SYSTEM

The objective is to ensure that an intelligent robotic system can distinguish between its theoretical capabilities and the capabilities supported by its actual installed hardware.

These integrations remain subject to separate implementation, testing, authorization, and validation.
