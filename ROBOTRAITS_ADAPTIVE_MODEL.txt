HARDWARE AND CAPABILITIES

Sensors may support perception.

Actuators may support movement.

Robotic hands may support manipulation.

End effectors may support specialized tasks.

Hardware changes may require capability reassessment and renewed testing.

BEHAVIOR CONFIGURATION

Separate core personality configuration from learned user preferences.

Example:

Core personality: Patient Learned preference: User prefers slower interaction Context: Conversation Result: Adjusted interaction pace

This represents configurable behavior and does not imply consciousness.

CROSS-PLATFORM TRANSFER

Assess hardware, sensors, actuators, control systems, dimensions, capabilities, safety constraints and environmental differences before transferring learned models or behaviors between robots.
